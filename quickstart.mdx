Baza runs inside Obsidian—a free, local-first Markdown editor—and extends it with AI agent automation, session lifecycle management, and cloud mirroring. This guide walks you from a blank machine to a fully operational vault. Most steps take less than a minute each.

## What Happens Next

After your first session, your `00-Inbox` note is queued for processing. The next time an agent runs against your vault, it will:

1. Read your note and extract entities, topics, and relationships.
2. Inject structured YAML frontmatter based on the tags and content.
3. Move the processed note from `00-Inbox` to the correct `02-Library/[Topic]_K` folder.
4. Log all changes to the weekly Baza Activity Log so you can review exactly what was done.

You can check the Activity Log at any time from your dashboard to see where your notes landed and how they were connected to the rest of the knowledge graph.
